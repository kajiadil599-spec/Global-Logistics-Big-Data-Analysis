Global E-Commerce Logistics & Profitability Analysis
Project Goal: To identify shipping bottlenecks and profitability leaks across a global supply chain of 50,000+ records.

🛠️ Technical Workflow
Data Engineering (Python/Colab): Simulated 50k rows of complex logistics data; performed feature engineering to create 'Profit Margin' and 'Delivery Delay' metrics.

Data Modeling (Power BI): Developed a Star Schema model to link regional performance with financial outcomes.

Visualization: Built an 8-chart interactive dashboard focusing on KPI tracking and root-cause analysis.

📊 Key Insights Generated
Shipping Efficiency: Identified that 'Air Priority' shipments in the 'Asia-Pacific' region have a 12% higher delay rate than 'Land Transport'.

Profitability Outliers: Discovered that while 'Electronics' drive 40% of revenue, 'Healthcare' products maintain a 5% higher net profit margin.

Risk Management: Established a "Tipping Point" where shipping costs exceeding $400 significantly erode margins on low-quantity orders.
